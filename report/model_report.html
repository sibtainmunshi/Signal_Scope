Task and scope

Binary real vs AI-generated image classification with a continuous AI-positive score; general objects, scenes and products only. Built: core detector, A (model-linked explanation + audit), C (robustness), D (EXIF only; C2PA not checked), F (local web app/CLI), G (bounded post-processing attack analysis).

Audit found real JPEG vs generated square PNG in both GenImage and the external set, so training balances JPEG and every external result is also reported format-matched (identical crop, resize and JPEG for both labels).

Model and training

  • ImageNet-pretrained ResNet-18, fine-tuned end to end (AdamW 2e-4, cosine, 10 epochs, batch 64, AMP); epoch chosen by mean GenImage/CIFAKE val AUC.
  • Native-resolution random 128 px crops (no resizing); every generated training image JPEG-compressed with qualities drawn from the real photos; symmetric rescale, blur, flip and crop-JPEG augmentation.
  • Inference: mean logit of up to five native 128 px crops; CPU ~15–50 ms per image on our laptop.
  • Temperature 1.65 fit on calibration splits; threshold 0.455 = strictest validation threshold with real FPR ≤ 5.0% per domain. ECE CIFAKE 0.027→0.009, GenImage 0.047→0.061 (worse).

Explanation (Module A)

Returned-class Grad-CAM per crop, stitched, plus a masking diagnostic. 40 fixed dev images (27 correct): masking the top Grad-CAM window lowered the verdict score more than random windows in 72.5% (mean fill, p=0.002) and 67.5% (blur); effects are small. JPEG map stability median rho 0.95; weight-randomization rho 0.52 (maps only partly weight-dependent). No named artifact (text, lighting, anatomy) is claimed.

Robustness (Module C/G)

GenImage val AUC original 0.956; JPEG q70 0.953; q30 0.906; half resolution 0.896; blur 0.937. Six-transformation search flipped 19.8% of initially correct predictions; on 32 px CIFAKE, half resolution drops AUC to 0.726 (flip rate 42.6%).

Limitations and failure cases

  • Unseen-generator AUC is modest. On the reserved unseen set the mean AUC is 0.565 / 0.643 (as distributed / format-matched); GLIDE is weakest. At the conservative threshold most unseen AI images are missed.
  • Pristine PNG outputs are harder than JPEG-compressed ones: training JPEG-balances generated images, so the model does not reward "no JPEG" as evidence of AI.
  • Calibration is domain-specific: confidence and FPR need not transfer to new generators or pipelines.
  • Resizing, blur and strong JPEG raise real-image false positives; tiny upscaled images are fragile.
  • Grad-CAM shows model influence, not verified visual defects; audit sample is small and unannotated.
